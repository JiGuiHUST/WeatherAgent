{{ t('safety.weather') }}
    {% if is_en %}
  • Monitor GOWFS 08/20 updates; beyond 7 days, use climate averages
  • Avoid island-hopping in heavy rain; respect red-flag beach warnings
  • Stay hydrated; avoid 11:00-15:00 sun; carry sunscreen & electrolytes
  • Thunderstorms: stay away from trees, coasts, high ground, metal gear
    • {% else %}
  • 关注 08/20 时 GOWFS 预报更新，超 7 天时效用气候均值参考
  • 暴雨时避开海岛跳岛、海岸活动，留意红旗警示
  • 高温时段避开正午烈日，备防晒、补水、电解质饮料
  • 雷暴天气远离大树、海岸、高地与金属装备
    • {% endif %}
{{ t('safety.transit') }}
    {% if is_en %}
  • Verify IDP & cross-border insurance before driving abroad
  • Helmets mandatory for scooters; check local traffic side
  • Prefer ride-hailing over street taxis; share trip with family
  • Avoid overnight buses & overloaded vehicles
    • {% else %}
  • 跨境自驾核查保险、驾照国际认证（IDP）有效期
  • 摩托车/电瓶车必戴头盔，确认当地行驶方向
  • 打车用网约车平台，上车核对车牌并分享行程
  • 避开夜间山路、超载车辆与黑车
    • {% endif %}
{{ t('safety.health') }}
    {% if is_en %}
  • Mosquito protection: DEET repellent, long sleeves; dengue endemic
  • Bottled/boiled water only; avoid raw food & street ice
  • SPF50+ sunscreen; hat & sunglasses; limit midday sun
  • Pack: antidiarrheal, antipyretic, antihistamine, personal meds
    • {% else %}
  • 防蚊：备 DEET 驱蚊液、长袖衣物，东南亚登革热流行
  • 饮水安全：瓶装水/煮沸水，避免生冷食物与街边冰块
  • 防晒：SPF50+ 防晒霜、墨镜、宽檐帽，避开正午烈日
  • 备药：肠胃药、退烧药、抗过敏药及个人慢性病用药
    • {% endif %}
{{ t('safety.docs') }}
    {% if is_en %}
  • Separate copies from originals; scan & upload to cloud
  • Split cash across pockets; avoid flashy brands & luxury goods
  • Backpack in front in crowds; watch for distractions & groups
  • Use hotel safe; carry cards from 2+ different issuers
    • {% else %}
  • 证件复印件与原件分开存放，电子版上传云盘备份
  • 现金分多口袋存放，少带名牌奢侈品，避免露财
  • 街边防扒：双肩包前背，警惕搭讪与群聚围观
  • 酒店保险柜存贵重物品，信用卡备两张不同银行的
    • {% endif %}
{{ t('safety.border') }}
    {% if is_en %}
  • Passport validity ≥ 6 months; ≥ 2 blank pages
  • Match visa type with trip; prepare return ticket & hotel booking
  • Declare cash over USD 10,000 equivalent; check customs rules
  • Stay calm at immigration; answer truthfully without hesitation
    • {% else %}
  • 护照有效期 ≥ 6 个月，空白页 ≥ 2 页
  • 签证类型与入境目的相符，落地签备返程机票与酒店订单
  • 等值 USD 10000 以上现金需申报；查询目的国违禁品
  • 入境被问询保持冷静如实回答，避免东张西望
    • {% endif %}
{{ t('safety.emergency') }}
    {% if is_en %}
  • 12308 China consular hotline (24h); save embassy addresses
  • Buy insurance with medical evacuation & cross-border rescue
  • Daily check-in with family; share itinerary & hotel info
  • Prioritize personal safety; report loss post-incident to embassy
    • {% else %}
  • 中国领事保护热线 12308（24h），留存使领馆地址电话
  • 购买含医疗运送、跨境救援的旅行保险并留存保单号
  • 与家人约定每日联络时段，留存酒店地址与行程单
  • 遇险优先保障人身安全，事后联系使领馆报案
    • {% endif %}

{{ t('safety.children') }}
    {% if is_en %}
  • Choose destinations with good hygiene and easy transportation
  • Avoid strenuous activities like mountain climbing or trekking
  • Strict food safety: no raw food, unpeeled fruit, or street ice
  • Sun protection essential; wide-brim hat, sunscreen SPF50+
  • Ensure up-to-date vaccinations before departure
    • {% else %}
  • 选择卫生条件好、交通方便的景点，避开探险类活动
  • 不宜进行登山、探险等剧烈户外运动
  • 注意饮食卫生，不吃生冷食物与街边冰块
  • 备遮阳帽或防晒伞，防晒指数 SPF50+
  • 出发前检查疫苗接种是否完整
    • {% endif %}
{{ t('safety.elderly') }}
    {% if is_en %}
  • Manage blood pressure & chronic conditions; carry sufficient medication
  • Avoid extreme temperature changes; pack layers for both hot and cold
  • Choose stable, wide-footwear; avoid slippery paths & stairs
  • Schedule rest periods; avoid overexertion in heat & humidity
  • Travel insurance with medical evacuation is strongly recommended
    • {% else %}
  • 按时服用慢性病药物，携带充足储备
  • 防止温差剧变影响健康，备薄外套
  • 选择防滑稳健的鞋子，避开湿滑台阶
  • 行程中安排休息时段，避免高温时段过度劳累
  • 强烈建议购买含医疗运送的旅行保险
    • {% endif %}
{{ t('safety.pregnant') }}
    {% if is_en %}
  • Obtain prenatal checkup before departure; know your health status
  • Wear loose clothes, non-slip shoes; avoid damp/cold seating
  • Choose short-haul trips; maintain work-rest balance, avoid fatigue
  • Pick transport with restroom; choose aisle seat for easy access
  • Immediate medical care if abdominal pain or bleeding occurs
    • {% else %}
  • 出发前做孕期检查，掌握胎儿和自身健康状况
  • 穿宽松衣、防滑鞋，不坐湿冷地方
  • 选择短程旅行，保持劳逸均衡
  • 选有洗手间的交通工具，靠走道座位
  • 出现腹痛、出血立即就医
    • {% endif %}
{# —— 东南亚特色活动安全 —— #}

{{ t('safety.activity') }}

{{ t('safety.activity.sub') }}

{{ t('safety.notes') }}
    {% if is_en %}
  • Choose licensed operators with life jackets & insurance; avoid unregulated boatmen
  • Check weather before departure; cancel if red flag or strong wind warning
  • Wear life jacket at all times; children must wear size-appropriate jackets
  • Stay near the boat; don't swim to isolated reefs or rocks
  • Beware of rip currents; if caught, swim parallel to shore, not against
  • Drink bottled water; avoid consuming local water or raw seafood from unknown vendors
    • {% else %}
  • 选择有资质的运营商，确认救生衣配备齐全、有保险
  • 出发前查看天气和海况；红旗或大风预警时取消行程
  • 全程穿救生衣，儿童必须使用合适尺码
  • 不要远离船只，不要游向孤立的礁石
  • 警惕离岸流；若被卷入，顺流平行游回岸边
  • 饮用瓶装水，避免食用不明来源的海鲜
    • {% endif %}
{{ t('safety.firstaid') }}
    {% if is_en %}
  • Drowning: check consciousness, open airway, rescue breathing & chest compression (30:2)
  • Jellyfish sting: rinse with vinegar (not fresh water), remove tentacles, take pain relief
  • Dehydration: move to shade, sip oral rehydration solution, avoid ice water
  • Keep injured person warm and seek medical attention as soon as possible
    • {% else %}
  • 溺水：检查意识、开放气道、人工呼吸+胸外按压（30:2）
  • 水母蜇伤：用醋冲洗（不用淡水），清除触手，服用止痛药
  • 中暑脱水：移至阴凉处，小口补充口服补液盐，忌冰水
  • 受伤人员保持保暖，尽快就医
    • {% endif %}

{{ t('safety.notes') }}
    {% if is_en %}
  • Snorkeling: buddy system; never snorkel alone; check mask seal before entering water
  • Diving: always use certified operator; ensure DAN insurance; do not dive beyond certification depth
  • Follow dive table strictly; do not fly within 24 hours after diving
  • Watch for jellyfish, sea urchins, fire coral; wear protective gear
  • Avoid touching or standing on coral; it's fragile and can cause cuts
  • Surface immediately if you feel disoriented or short of breath
    • {% else %}
  • 浮潜：结伴同行，不单独下水；下水前检查面罩密封性
  • 潜水：选择 PADI/SSI 认证机构，确保 DAN 保险，不超执照深度
  • 严格遵守潜水表，潜水后 24 小时内不乘坐飞机
  • 注意水母、海胆、火珊瑚，穿戴防护装备
  • 不触摸珊瑚，不踩踏珊瑚，珊瑚易断且会刺伤
  • 出现头晕、呼吸困难立即上浮
    • {% endif %}
{{ t('safety.firstaid') }}
    {% if is_en %}
  • Cuts from coral: clean with fresh water, apply antiseptic, monitor for infection
  • Decompression sickness: immediate hyperbaric chamber treatment; do NOT delay
  • Panic in water: signal buddy, float on back, breathe slowly; do NOT swim aimlessly
  • Seasickness: take medication 1 hour before departure, stay hydrated
    • {% else %}
  • 珊瑚划伤：淡水清洁伤口，涂消毒剂，注意感染
  • 减压病：立即送高压氧舱治疗，不可延误
  • 水中惊慌：示意同伴，仰面漂浮，缓慢呼吸
  • 晕船：出发前 1 小时服药，注意补水
    • {% endif %}

{{ t('safety.notes') }}
    {% if is_en %}
  • Always go with a local guide; never trek alone in unfamiliar jungle
  • Wear long pants and long sleeves to protect against leeches and thorns
  • Use insect repellent (DEET); check body for ticks/leeches every hour
  • Stay on marked trails; if lost, stay put and signal for help
  • Carry: sufficient water, energy food, first aid kit, whistle, compass
  • Beware of wildlife: do not feed or approach monkeys, snakes, or other animals
    • {% else %}
  • 必须跟随当地向导，不单独进入陌生丛林
  • 穿长裤长袖，防水蛭、荆棘和蚊虫叮咬
  • 使用 DEET 驱蚊液，每小时检查身体有无寄生虫
  • 走标记路线，迷路时原地不动，发出求救信号
  • 必备：充足饮水、能量食物、急救包、口哨、指南针
  • 不投喂野生动物，远离猴子、蛇等动物
    • {% endif %}
{{ t('safety.firstaid') }}
    {% if is_en %}
  • Leech bite: do not pull off; apply salt or alcohol to make it drop; clean wound
  • Snake bite: keep calm, immobilize limb, do NOT try to suck venom; seek medical help immediately
  • Heat exhaustion: rest in shade, sip electrolyte solution, fan and cool with damp cloth
  • Deep cut: apply direct pressure with clean cloth; elevate limb; seek medical attention
    • {% else %}
  • 水蛭叮咬：不要硬拉，用盐或酒精刺激使其脱落，清洁伤口
  • 蛇咬伤：保持冷静，固定伤肢，不要吸毒液，立即就医
  • 中暑：阴凉处休息，补充电解质溶液，用湿布降温
  • 深切割伤：干净布块直接加压止血，抬高患肢，就医
    • {% endif %}

{{ t('safety.notes') }}
    {% if is_en %}
  • Check boat condition; avoid overloaded or unseaworthy vessels
  • Life jackets must be available and worn; know their location on board
  • Listen to safety briefing; identify emergency exits & muster points
  • Mind the gap when boarding/alighting; watch your step on wet decks
  • River rafting: wear helmet and life jacket; follow guide instructions
  • Do not stand up or lean over railings while boat is moving
    • {% else %}
  • 检查船只状况，不乘坐超载或破损的船只
  • 确认救生衣可用，了解存放位置并全程穿戴
  • 听取安全讲解，了解紧急出口和集合点
  • 上下船注意缝隙，湿滑甲板小心行走
  • 漂流时戴头盔穿救生衣，听从向导指挥
  • 船只行驶中不站立、不倚靠栏杆
    • {% endif %}
{{ t('safety.firstaid') }}
    {% if is_en %}
  • Man overboard: shout "MAN OVERBOARD", throw life ring, do NOT jump in; inform captain
  • Hypothermia: move to warm area, remove wet clothes, wrap in blankets; do NOT rub skin
  • Burns from hot drinks/engine: cool with running water for 10+ minutes; do NOT apply ointment
  • Seasickness: lie down, close eyes, breathe deeply; antiemetics available
    • {% else %}
  • 落水：呼喊"有人落水"，投掷救生圈，不要跳水，通知船长
  • 失温：移至温暖处，脱湿衣，毛毯包裹，不要揉搓皮肤
  • 烫伤：流动冷水冲洗 10 分钟以上，不涂药膏
  • 晕船：躺下闭眼深呼吸，可用止吐药
    • {% endif %}

{{ t('safety.notes') }}
    {% if is_en %}
  • Dress modestly: shoulders and knees covered; carry a sarong for shoulder covering
  • Remove shoes before entering temple; do not step on door threshold with feet
  • Keep voice low; no loud talking or flash photography inside sanctuaries
  • Do not touch Buddha images or sacred objects; do not sit with feet pointing to Buddha
  • Be cautious of scammers offering "free" blessings or requiring donations
  • Respect local customs; do not show affection in public near temples
    • {% else %}
  • 着装端庄：遮肩盖膝，随身带纱丽披肩
  • 进寺脱鞋，不踩门槛
  • 保持安静，殿内不大声喧哗、不用闪光灯拍照
  • 不触摸佛像，不脚对佛像坐姿
  • 警惕"免费"祈福或强制捐款的骗局
  • 尊重当地习俗，寺庙附近不公开亲密行为
    • {% endif %}
{{ t('safety.firstaid') }}
    {% if is_en %}
  • Heat stroke in temple: move to shade, fan, sip water; temple is often hot and crowded
  • Cuts from sharp stairs/statues: clean and bandage; watch for tetanus risk
  • Fainting due to heat/dehydration: lay down, elevate legs, sponge with cool water
    • {% else %}
  • 寺庙内中暑：移至阴凉处，扇风补水，寺庙通常闷热拥挤
  • 锋利台阶/雕像划伤：清洁包扎，注意破伤风风险
  • 高温脱水昏厥：躺下抬腿，凉水擦拭降温
    • {% endif %}

{{ t('safety.notes') }}
    {% if is_en %}
  • Carry small change; use a secure money belt; avoid displaying large sums
  • Bargain respectfully; never show anger; walk away if price is too high
  • Watch belongings in crowds; use a cross-body bag or wear backpack in front
  • Drink only sealed bottled water; avoid raw juices from street vendors
  • Check fruit/dessert is fresh; avoid food left at room temperature for long
  • Be aware of tourist scams: "gem special", "tailor discount", etc.
    • {% else %}
  • 带零钱，用安全腰包，不露财
  • 礼貌砍价，不生气，价格不合适就走
  • 人多的地方看好随身物品，用斜挎包或前背双肩包
  • 只喝密封瓶装水，不喝街头鲜榨果汁
  • 注意水果/甜品新鲜度，不吃长时间室温放置的食物
  • 警惕旅游陷阱："宝石特价"、"裁缝折扣"等
    • {% endif %}
{{ t('safety.firstaid') }}
    {% if is_en %}
  • Food poisoning: drink rehydration solution; seek medical care if severe
  • Cut from broken glass/spice: clean with water; apply pressure bandage
  • Lost in crowd: find designated meeting point; do NOT panic; ask vendor/stall owner for help
    • {% else %}
  • 食物中毒：喝补液盐，严重时就医
  • 玻璃/香料割伤：清水清洁，加压包扎
  • 人群中走散：找约定集合点，不慌张，找摊主求助
    • {% endif %}
